# Accounts

Sign-in with Google and Discord through Supabase Auth, the pages of a signed-in visitor and the friends' panel (PLT-16, PLT-17, PLT-23). What exists, what is still a placeholder, and what has to be set up in Supabase before a visitor can sign in.

## What exists
- `@pbgo/accounts` (`packages/accounts`): `.` the pure part (`Account`, `accountOf`, `safeNext`, `PROVIDERS`, and the figures of a profile: `statsOf`, `perGame`, `ordinal`), `./config` (the project: `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`, the two variables the games already read; without them the hub has no sign-in and says so), `./client` (`useAccount`, `signInWith`), `./server` (`createAuthClient`, `getAccount`, `requireAccount`), `./proxy` (`updateSession`), `./demo`. The app imports it; it never imports core or a game
- An account is what the provider says of the visitor (`accountOf`): Google gives `full_name` and `avatar_url`, Discord `custom_claims.global_name` (the name they chose, not their login) and `avatar_url`; the nickname falls back to the start of the e-mail, then to "Player". Nothing is stored beside Supabase Auth's own `auth.users`: no `profiles` table, no nickname tag, no settings (PLT-13, PLT-39)
- Session: Supabase Auth's cookies (`@supabase/ssr`), the same in the browser and on the server. A page that is for a signed-in visitor asks the Auth server (`getUser()`, never the cookie alone: anyone can write a cookie); `apps/web/src/proxy.ts` (Next 16's `proxy`, the former middleware) renews an expired session before the page renders, and only for `/me/*` and `/login`, so every public page and every game stays static. A visitor who is not signed in and asks for `/me/*` goes to `/login?next=<the page>` (the proxy, then `requireAccount` as a second check)
- Routes (`apps/web`): `/login` (`(hub)/login`: a button per provider on the hub's backdrop; `?next=` is where to go back to, `?error=signin` a sign-in that did not work; a visitor already signed in is sent on), `/auth/callback` (the provider's way back: it exchanges the one-time `code` for a session and redirects to `next`), `/logout` (POST only: it ends the session and redirects to `/`; a form posts to it, never a link), `/auth/demo` (development only, below), and the pages of an account `/me` (the profile: figures, the games one by one, the last games), `/me/games` (in progress, then history) and `/me/friends` (add a friend, requests, and the list where the side panel has no room). They are `noindex`, disallowed in `robots.txt`, and not in the sitemap
- `safeNext` is the one way back from a sign-in: a value anyone can write into a link is a path of this site (one slash, no backslash, no control character, not the sign-in's own way) or it is `/me`
- The navbar (`SiteHeader`, a client component on static pages) reads the session in the browser (`useAccount`): the server renders every page as if nobody were signed in, so the first paint is the same everywhere. Until the browser has read its session the two account buttons keep their place but are not drawn (`invisible`), so a signed-in visitor never sees them flash; then Sign in (to `/login?next=<this page>`) and Let's play, or `SiteSocial` (`apps/web`): the bar of the games the visitor's friends are in (`FriendsBar`) and, in the account's island, the visitor's picture with a count of what waits for them (messages not read, requests to answer), which opens the social panel (`SocialPanel`, on the right of the page: the friends and what they are doing, the conversations, the way to add a friend with the requests). The menu of the account (`AccountMenu`: name, Profile, My games, Friends, Sign out) is under their name at the top of the panel. A press on a friend or a conversation opens the chat with them (`ChatWindow`), where a message sent is kept for as long as the page is open
- The frame of the pages of an account (`AccountShell`): the page in a column that does not stretch with the window, between the navbar and the footer. The friends are not in it: they are the navbar's, on every page, and `/me/friends` lists them in full (`FriendsList`, the `FriendsPanel` of `@pbgo/ui`, with its search). `/design` draws the friends' panel, bar and social panel (sections `friends-panel`, `friends-bar`, `social-panel`)

## What is a placeholder
- A real account has no friends, no conversations, no requests and no games: the pages and the panel say so with an empty state. The games (the tables, the results) and the friends are in the platform schema of PLT-13 (`tables`, `game_results`, `players`) and PLT-23; until they exist `apps/web/src/lib/account-data.ts` (the pages) and `apps/web/src/lib/use-social.ts` (the panel, in the browser) return nothing for a real account. The buttons that need them (add a friend, invite, join, accept) are not drawn for a real account, and do nothing for the demo account
- What works is what needs no friend to exist: the panel's tabs and search, "Invite a friend" (it copies the address of the sign-in), and the chat window, whose messages stay for as long as the page is open: messages have no backend yet (PLT-23). Joining, inviting, sending and answering a request do nothing but for the demo account, and are not drawn for a real one
- Not there yet: the settings page (nickname, picture, language, sound, hide my history: PLT-16), the nickname with a tag (PLT-39), a guest's past games attached on sign-up (PLT-38), the signed-in state in a game (PLT-17), account deletion (PLT-36), the admin through the account (PLT-41). The legal text (`/legal#cookies`, `/privacy`, `/terms`) does not mention the sign-in's cookies yet: that is for the owner to write

## Setting it up (once for each Supabase project: `pbgo-preview`, then production)
The code needs no new variable. A visitor can only sign in once the providers are enabled in the project (Authentication › Providers) and its redirect URLs allow the hub:
1. **Google**: in Google Cloud Console, APIs & Services › Credentials, create an OAuth client ID of type Web application; its authorised redirect URI is `https://<project ref>.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback`. Put its client ID and secret in the Google provider of the project and enable it
2. **Discord**: in the Discord Developer Portal, a new application, OAuth2, add the same redirect `https://<project ref>.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback`; put its client ID and secret in the Discord provider and enable it
3. **URL configuration** (Authentication › URL Configuration): Site URL `https://playboardgamesonline.app`, and the redirect URLs `https://playboardgamesonline.app/auth/callback`, `http://localhost:3100/auth/callback` and, for the previews, the pattern of the Vercel preview addresses followed by `/auth/callback`
4. `pnpm setup-env` fills `apps/web/.env.local` with the preview project's URL and publishable key (they are the same two variables the games use). A checkout without them shows the sign-in page with its buttons off

## The demo account (development only)
`/auth/demo?next=/me` signs a made-up account in (a cookie, `pbgo_demo_account`; `/auth/demo?off=1` signs it out) to see the pages of a signed-in visitor on a machine with no provider set up. It has a picture, friends, requests, conversations and games of its own (`account-data.ts`, `demo-data.ts`; the pictures are `demoAvatar`, an emoji on a colour drawn as an SVG data URI, so that nothing is a file and it works offline). A build answers 404 to the route and ignores the cookie (`demoAllowed()` reads `NODE_ENV`, which the build inlines), and nothing about it is stored anywhere

## Rules
- A page that shows an account is rendered per request (it reads cookies): keep that to `/login` and `/me/*`. The navbar and every other page of the hub ask the browser, never the server
- Never trust what the browser holds: the server asks the Auth server, and a redirect target goes through `safeNext`
- Platform UI only: the pages use `@pbgo/ui` components and tokens, and a new visual component goes into `@pbgo/ui` first, with its section on `/design`
