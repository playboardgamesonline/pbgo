# Accounts

Sign-in with a password, or with Facebook, Google, Apple and Discord, through Supabase Auth, the pages of a signed-in visitor and the friends' panel (PLT-16, PLT-17, PLT-23). What exists, what is still a placeholder, and what has to be set up in Supabase before a visitor can sign in.

## What exists
- `@pbgo/accounts` (`packages/accounts`): `.` the pure part (`Account`, `accountOf`, `safeNext`, `PROVIDERS`, and the figures of a profile: `statsOf`, `perGame`, `ordinal`), `./config` (the project: `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`, the two variables the games already read; without them the hub has no sign-in and says so), `./client` (`useAccount`, `signInWith`), `./server` (`createAuthClient`, `getAccount`, `requireAccount`), `./proxy` (`updateSession`), `./demo`. The app imports it; it never imports core or a game
- An account is what the provider says of the visitor (`accountOf`): Google and Facebook give `full_name` and `avatar_url`, Discord `custom_claims.global_name` (the name they chose, not their login) and `avatar_url`, and Apple's sign-in on the web gives neither a name nor a picture; the nickname falls back to the start of the e-mail (never an Apple relay address, `…@privaterelay.appleid.com`, which says nothing of the person), then to "Player". Nothing is stored beside Supabase Auth's own `auth.users`: no `profiles` table, no nickname tag, no settings (PLT-13, PLT-39)
- Session: Supabase Auth's cookies (`@supabase/ssr`), the same in the browser and on the server. A page that is for a signed-in visitor asks the Auth server (`getUser()`, never the cookie alone: anyone can write a cookie); `apps/web/src/proxy.ts` (Next 16's `proxy`, the former middleware) renews an expired session before the page renders, and only for `/me/*` and `/login`, so every public page and every game stays static. A visitor who is not signed in and asks for `/me/*` goes to `/login?next=<the page>` (the proxy, then `requireAccount` as a second check)
- Routes (`apps/web`): `/login` (`(hub)/login`: a username and a password (`signInWithPassword`: the first field takes an e-mail address, `emailOf`, since Supabase Auth knows a visitor by their address alone and a username has nowhere to be looked up until the profiles exist, PLT-39; a username is told to use the address), then, under "or continue with", the four providers as four small buttons side by side, each with nothing on it but its mark (`ProviderButton`, `@pbgo/ui`), on the hub's backdrop; `?next=` is where to go back to, `?error=signin` a sign-in that did not work; a visitor already signed in is sent on), `/auth/callback` (the provider's way back: it exchanges the one-time `code` for a session and redirects to `next`), `/logout` (POST only: it ends the session and redirects to `/`; a form posts to it, never a link), `/auth/demo` (development only, below), and the pages of an account `/me` (the profile: figures, the games one by one, the last games), `/me/games` (in progress, then history) and `/me/friends` (add a friend, requests, and the list where the side panel has no room). They are `noindex`, disallowed in `robots.txt`, and not in the sitemap
- `safeNext` is the one way back from a sign-in: a value anyone can write into a link is a path of this site (one slash, no backslash, no control character, not the sign-in's own way) or it is `/me`
- The navbar (`SiteHeader`, a client component on static pages) reads the session in the browser (`useAccount`): the server renders every page as if nobody were signed in, so the first paint is the same everywhere. Until the browser has read its session the two account buttons keep their place but are not drawn (`invisible`), so a signed-in visitor never sees them flash; then Sign in (to `/login?next=<this page>`) and Let's play, or `SiteSocial` (`apps/web`): one container on the right, the dock (`SocialDock`), that is a bar while it is closed and a real side panel while it is open, the same element growing and shrinking between the two. The bar has the games the visitor's friends are in (`FriendsBar`, three pills at most), then, behind a thin line, the visitor's picture, with a count of what waits for them on its corner (`AvatarCount`: messages not read, requests to answer), which opens the menu of the account (`AccountMenu`: their picture big, with the dot of their presence, their name and the word that says it, then Profile, My games, Friends, Sign out), and the button of the panel (`SocialPanelToggle`: `PanelRightOpenIcon`, then `PanelRightCloseIcon` once it is open). Open, the bar stays at the top of the container and is only a recap (a press on a game takes the visitor to it in the list), and the panel (`SocialPanel`, 20rem wide, down to the bottom of the window) is under it, in three tabs: the friends game by game (each game with its picture, its name and how many of them are in it, the ones in its lobby and the ones in a game mixed), the conversations, and the way to add a friend with the requests. The tile of the current tab glides to the one that is pressed and its pane comes in from that side. In the conversations what is new is white and what was read is grey. A press on a friend or a conversation opens the chat with them (`ChatWindow`, next to the dock, sliding in from the bottom, with a `···` menu: join or invite them, remove them, block them), where a message sent is kept for as long as the page is open
- The frame of the pages of an account (`AccountShell`): the page in a column that does not stretch with the window, between the navbar and the footer. The friends are not in it: they are the navbar's, on every page, and `/me/friends` lists them in full (`FriendsList`, the `FriendsPanel` of `@pbgo/ui`, with its search). `/design` draws the friends' panel, bar and social panel with the dock that holds them (sections `friends-panel`, `friends-bar`, `social-panel`)

## What is a placeholder
- A real account has no friends, no conversations, no requests and no games: the pages and the panel say so with an empty state. The games (the tables, the results) and the friends are in the platform schema of PLT-13 (`tables`, `game_results`, `players`) and PLT-23; until they exist `apps/web/src/lib/account-data.ts` (the pages) and `apps/web/src/lib/use-social.ts` (the panel, in the browser) return nothing for a real account. The buttons that need them (add a friend, invite, join, accept) are not drawn for a real account, and do nothing for the demo account
- What works is what needs no friend to exist: the panel's tabs and search, "Invite a friend" (it copies the address of the sign-in), and the chat window, whose messages stay for as long as the page is open: messages have no backend yet (PLT-23). Joining, inviting, sending and answering a request do nothing but for the demo account, and are not drawn for a real one
- Not there yet: the settings page (nickname, picture, language, sound, hide my history: PLT-16), the nickname with a tag (PLT-39), a guest's past games attached on sign-up (PLT-38), the signed-in state in a game (PLT-17), account deletion (PLT-36), the admin through the account (PLT-41). The legal text (`/legal#cookies`, `/privacy`, `/terms`) does not mention the sign-in's cookies yet: that is for the owner to write

## Setting it up (once for each Supabase project: `pbgo-preview`, then production)
The code needs no new variable. A visitor can only sign in once the providers are enabled in the project (Authentication › Providers) and its redirect URLs allow the hub. The password needs the Email provider (on by default; keep "Confirm email" on): there is no sign-up page or password reset yet, so an e-mail account can only be made by hand in the dashboard (Authentication › Users) until they exist. The others:
1. **Facebook**: at developers.facebook.com, create an app (the use case "Authenticate and request data from users with Facebook Login") and, in the settings of Facebook Login, put `https://<project ref>.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback` under Valid OAuth Redirect URIs. Under Use Cases › Authentication and Account Creation check that `public_profile` and `email` are listed (add `email` if it is not: Supabase needs the address). Put the App ID and the App Secret (Settings › Basic) in the Facebook provider of the project and enable it. Only people with a role on the app can sign in while it is in Development: to open it to everybody fill in its icon, the privacy policy URL (`https://playboardgamesonline.app/privacy`, still a stub: PLT-36) and the terms URL, then switch it to Live (Meta may ask to verify the `email` permission). A Facebook account with no confirmed e-mail cannot sign in
2. **Google**: in Google Cloud Console, APIs & Services › Credentials, create an OAuth client ID of type Web application; its authorised redirect URI is `https://<project ref>.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback`. Put its client ID and secret in the Google provider of the project and enable it
3. **Apple**: it needs the Apple Developer Program (about US$99 a year). In Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles create an App ID with Sign in with Apple, a Services ID (its identifier is the client ID) with Sign in with Apple configured for the domain `<project ref>.supabase.co` and the return URL `https://<project ref>.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback`, and a key with Sign in with Apple (download the `.p8` once, note its Key ID and the Team ID). The Apple provider of the project takes the Services ID and a secret, which is a JWT signed with that key. **Apple lets that secret live six months at most: generate a new one and paste it in every six months, or Apple sign-in stops working** (put a reminder in the calendar). On the web Apple gives no name and no picture, so an Apple account is "Player" with its initials until the settings page exists (PLT-16), and a visitor may hide their address (a `privaterelay.appleid.com` one)
4. **Discord**: in the Discord Developer Portal, a new application, OAuth2, add the same redirect `https://<project ref>.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback`; put its client ID and secret in the Discord provider and enable it
5. **URL configuration** (Authentication › URL Configuration): Site URL `https://playboardgamesonline.app`, and the redirect URLs `https://playboardgamesonline.app/auth/callback`, `http://localhost:3100/auth/callback` and, for the previews, the pattern of the Vercel preview addresses followed by `/auth/callback`
6. `pnpm setup-env` fills `apps/web/.env.local` with the preview project's URL and publishable key (they are the same two variables the games use). A checkout without them shows the sign-in page with its buttons off

## The demo account (development only)
`/auth/demo?next=/me` signs a made-up account in (a cookie, `pbgo_demo_account`; `/auth/demo?off=1` signs it out) to see the pages of a signed-in visitor on a machine with no provider set up. It has a picture, friends, requests, conversations and games of its own (`account-data.ts`, `demo-data.ts`; the pictures look like the avatars of random people on Discord: DiceBear avatars (illustrated faces, cartoons, pixel art, abstract gradients; CC0 styles only) drawn once and kept as data URIs, the account's own small one in `demo.ts` and the friends' and requests' in `demo-avatars.ts`, which only `/design` and the demo data import, so that nothing is a file, nothing is fetched and it works offline). A build answers 404 to the route and ignores the cookie (`demoAllowed()` reads `NODE_ENV`, which the build inlines), and nothing about it is stored anywhere

## Rules
- A page that shows an account is rendered per request (it reads cookies): keep that to `/login` and `/me/*`. The navbar and every other page of the hub ask the browser, never the server
- Never trust what the browser holds: the server asks the Auth server, and a redirect target goes through `safeNext`
- Platform UI only: the pages use `@pbgo/ui` components and tokens, and a new visual component goes into `@pbgo/ui` first, with its section on `/design`
