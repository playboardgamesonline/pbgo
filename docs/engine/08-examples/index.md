# Examples

Flip 7 and Courtisans are the engines to read beside the pages on [a new game](../07-new-game/index.md). Flip 7 is the one to copy: a lean state, a step that opens a copy of the state and closes it, and tests that play whole games. Courtisans is live, and its engine shows what Flip 7 does not need: data loaded from Sanity, a simultaneous opening, a hidden family and results with the game's own detail.

## Pages

- [Flip 7](01-flip-7.md): the files of its engine, then the catalogue and the lean state, the step, `waitingFor`, `autoPlayFor`, the options, the results and the tests.
- [Courtisans](02-courtisans.md): its files, then `SetupData`, determinism from the seed, the missions phase, the view, the results, its own `EngineError` and its debug commands.
