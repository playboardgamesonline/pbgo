# The game

What every game has, whatever its rules: players, turns and phases, actions, an end, parameters, data, a state and the views of it, and the contract that binds an engine to the app. A page is the engine's side of a piece; where the table draws it too, the page says so and links to it.

- [How a game runs](01-how-a-game-runs.md): who owns what, the life of an action, how a game starts.
- [Players](02-players.md): seats and ids, the viewer, who is out, the order around the table.
- [Turns](03-turns.md): who the game waits for, steps everybody answers, absent players, and who plays on the table.
- [Phases](04-phases.md): the steps a game goes through, the choices and the reactions inside them.
- [Actions](05-actions.md): what a player sends, what the server checks, what an engine refuses.
- [Victory and results](06-victory.md): when a game ends, who won, the objectives and the rounds.
- [Parameters](07-parameters.md): what a table can set, and what reaches the engine.
- [Game data](08-game-data.md): the cards, texts and pictures that are not rules.
- [State](09-state.md): what the server keeps of a game, and where its randomness comes from.
- [Views](10-views.md): what each player sees, and what is kept back.
- [The contract](11-the-contract.md): `GameDefinition`, field by field.

What lies on the table is in [Components](../02-components/index.md).
