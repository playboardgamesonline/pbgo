# Routing of `apps/web`

The rules behind the routes table of the root `CLAUDE.md` (layouts, route wrappers, 404 behaviour, games that are not playable, reserved slugs), then the rate limiting of the mutating routes.

## Routes (`apps/web/src/app`): the rules behind the table

- The hub (`(hub)/layout.tsx`: `/`, `/games`, `/game/<slug>`, `/tables`, `/tournaments`, `/pricing`, `/about`, `/legal`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/design`), the games (`[game]/layout.tsx`) and the gamemaster (`gamemaster/layout.tsx`: platform look, no game runtime, noindex) have separate root layouts; `global-not-found.tsx` answers any URL that matches no route. Links from the hub to a game are plain `<a>` (full page load).
- Route files are thin wrappers: pages call `@pbgo/core/pages/*` with the game (`gameOf` / `playableGameOf` / `previewableGameOf` from `apps/web/src/lib/game.ts`; the gamemaster pages take any game of the registry through `getGame`), API routes wrap the `@pbgo/core/routes/api/**` handler with `gameRoute()` from `@pbgo/games` and the `@pbgo/core/routes/gamemaster/**` ones with `gamemasterRoute()`. Segment configs (`dynamic`, `revalidate`) stay written out in the app (Next does not read them through a re-export).
- 404: a URL that matches no route (`/x/a/b/c`, `/<slug>/not-a-page`) gets `global-not-found.tsx`, rendered on the server. A page that calls `notFound()` (`gameOf` / `playableGameOf` / `previewableGameOf`: unknown slug, missing table or preview) gets `[game]/not-found.tsx` (`GameNotFound`): the game's own 404 for a slug of the registry, the site's 404 for any other slug. The gamemaster has its own `gamemaster/not-found.tsx` (unknown game or section), and the hub has `(hub)/not-found.tsx` for its own pages (a `/game/<slug>` the catalog does not list): the site's 404 inside the hub layout, whose title template adds the site name. Next 16.4 answers a page's `notFound()` with status 404 and `noindex` but renders the not-found UI on the client (the server HTML has an empty body).
- The `[game]` layout is a root layout, so it must not call `notFound()` itself: nothing sits above it to catch the error and Next answers with its own default 404 page. For an unknown slug it renders a bare `<html>` shell and each page's `gameOf` answers 404. `dynamicParams = false` is not enforced on these dynamic pages.
- A game that is not playable here (no engine, or not live in production) keeps its home page ("coming soon"); its table and API answer 404 (`GAME_NOT_FOUND`). The gamemaster ignores that rule: it manages every game of the registry, live or not, in production too, and its previews render any game with an engine (`hasEngine`, `getPreviewableGame`), for the signed-in gamemaster only.
- `apps/web/public` holds the platform's own files only: `favicon.ico`, the hub's icon. A game's pictures and sounds live in its Sanity project, never in the repo, so a game has no folder there and no `asset()`; `registry.test.ts` fails on any other file in `apps/web/public`, and so does the build (`scripts/check-public.mjs` runs before `next build` in `apps/web`, since a deployment never runs the tests; `pnpm check:public` runs it alone)
- `api`, `legal`, `privacy`, `terms`, `design`, `games`, `tables`, `tournaments`, `whats-new`, `pricing`, `about`, `ingest`, `gamemaster`, `me`, `auth` and `game` (the hub's page of a game, `/game/<slug>`, which is also the segment of every table's address, `/<slug>/game/<code>`) are routes of `apps/web`, `media` and `catalog` are the routes under `/api` that sit next to `/api/<slug>`, and `login` / `logout` are routes too (`/login`, `/logout`, and `/gamemaster/login` sits next to `/gamemaster/<slug>`), so none of them can be a game slug. `RESERVED_SLUGS` of `packages/games/src/slug.ts` is the one definition of what a slug and a name can be: the scripts (`pnpm new-game`, `pnpm add-game`), `registry.test.ts` and `slug.test.ts` all read it, and `slug.test.ts` keeps the list equal to the routes of `apps/web`
- `/api/catalog/<slug>/<logo|icon|background>` is not a game's API: it serves the pictures of every game of the catalog, live or not (files of its Notion row), through `pictureRoute()` of `@pbgo/catalog/route`, which its route file exports as `GET` the way `/api/media` exports `mediaRoute(...)`. The static segment `catalog` wins over `[game]`, so it never meets `/api/<slug>/games/<code>`. It is a GET that starts nothing a visitor can choose, so it has no `rateLimit` rule: the slot and the slug are checked against the catalog before anything is read, and a `v` that is not the row's current version is redirected before anything is downloaded (`docs/catalog.md`)
- `/game/<slug>` is the hub's page of a game (`(hub)/game/[slug]/page.tsx`), for every game of the catalog, live or to come. It has two segments and the table of a game three (`/<slug>/game/<code>`), and the static `game` wins over `[game]`, so the two never meet. The slug is looked up in the catalog (`getGamePage`, `apps/web/src/lib/game-page.ts`): a slug it does not list answers 404 with the hub's own `not-found.tsx`, and `/game` alone is no page. The page ends with the games the catalog relates to it (`related` of its entry), drawn as the home's cards. `robots.txt` has no rule for it (`/*/game/` needs a segment before `game`), so the page says itself whether it may be indexed (its `robots` meta), and the sitemap lists the ones that may (`docs/catalog.md`)
- The tabs of the hub's navbar (`SITE_TABS` in `apps/web/src/components/site-header.tsx`) each lead to a page of the hub. The hub's layout mounts the bar once (`SiteHeader`, a client component), and it reads the address to know what is current (`currentOf`: the logo on `/`, a tab on its page, Games on `/game/<slug>`, the bar and no tab on `/login` and the pages of an account, and nothing, so no bar, on `/design`, the legal pages and the 404); the pages do not draw it. Mounted once, it stays from one page to the next, which is what lets the fill behind the current item glide (`docs/design-system.md`, Motion). The logo is the home, `/`, and it is the current button there (the front page is no tab); Games is `/games` (a game's page, `/game/<slug>`, is Games too), the list of every game of the catalog, indexable and in the sitemap; Tables, Tournaments, Pricing and About are `/tables`, `/tournaments`, `/pricing` and `/about`, pages with a title and an empty state for now (About also holds a "What's new" section, `/about#whats-new`; `/whats-new`, the page it used to be, is a permanent redirect to it in `next.config.ts`, and stays a reserved slug) (`ComingSoonPage`: the title, what is to come, the footer), `noindex` and out of the sitemap. `/privacy` and `/terms`, which the footer links to, are `LegalStubPage`s (a title and a line that says the text is being written), `noindex`, until the owner has the text; the cookie policy is the cookies section of `/legal` (`/legal#cookies`). The sections of the home end with a "See all" that leads to the page of their tab. A new tab is a route folder in `(hub)`, an entry in `SITE_TABS` and a reserved slug (`slug.test.ts` fails without it)
- The accounts: `/login`, `/me/*` (per request: they read the session), `/auth/callback`, `/logout` and the development-only `/auth/demo`. `apps/web/src/proxy.ts` (Next 16's `proxy`) matches `/me/:path*` and `/login` only: it renews the session and sends a visitor who is not signed in to `/login?next=…`, so no public page and no game is touched by it. Their robots rules are in `robots.ts`. See `docs/accounts.md`

## Rate limiting
- Every mutating game route (create, join, leave, options, start, action, replay, takeover) and `POST /api/<slug>/feedback` start with `await rateLimit(game.slug, request, "<rule>")` (`server/rate-limit.ts`). Limits live in `RATE_LIMITS` (`server/rate-limiter.ts`): per player cookie when present, else per IP (create: always per IP), never per table, plus a per-IP ceiling across routes. Over the limit: 429 `RATE_LIMITED` + `Retry-After`. The chat route has its own per-player token bucket
- `POST /gamemaster/api/login` belongs to no game: it starts with `await rateLimit(null, request, "adminLogin")`, 5 attempts per 10 minutes per IP, counted before the credentials are checked (the right password is refused too once the limit is reached)
- Counters are in memory, per function instance; to share them, implement `RateLimitStore` and call `setRateLimitStore()`. A new mutating route of a game (`ROUTES`) must call `rateLimit` too
