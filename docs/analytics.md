# Analytics

PostHog through `@pbgo/analytics`: typed events, the consent banner, recordings, masking and the scrubbing of table codes.

## Analytics (PostHog, `@pbgo/analytics`)
Optional: without `NEXT_PUBLIC_POSTHOG_KEY` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_POSTHOG_HOST` nothing runs (no request, no banner, no "Cookies" link). Vercel's `<Analytics />` is a separate, cookie-free measure and is unchanged. Every event carries `env` (`production`, `preview` or `development`: `VERCEL_ENV`, copied to `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_ENV` by `next.config.ts`), the property that tells a real player from a test
- Events are typed in `packages/analytics/src/events.ts` (snake_case, `game` = the slug on every game event) and hold no personal data: never a nickname, chat text or table code. A new event = one entry there, then one line at the call site
- Server events (counted whatever the visitor answered): `trackAfter(event, playerId, properties)` of `@pbgo/core/server/analytics`, in `packages/core/routes/api/games/**`, after `updateGame` returns and never inside its modifier (that runs again when it loses a race). The id is the random value of the game's `<slug>_player` cookie, so nothing more is stored on the device; `identity_stable: false` until accounts exist (PLT-13), no person profile, no geolocation. `game_finished` is sent when the returned row is `over`; its `duration_s` and `moves_count` are approximations (`updated_at − created_at`, `version`) flagged `approximate: true`. Not tracked yet: `game_abandoned` (needs a sweeper)
- Browser: `AnalyticsProvider` is mounted by the hub, `[game]` and `global-not-found` root layouts, never by the gamemaster. The SDK talks to `/ingest` (rewritten to PostHog's EU hosts, `next.config.ts`; `skipTrailingSlashRedirect` is global because PostHog's endpoints end with a slash, and every canonical URL and the sitemap are explicit strings without one). Before the visitor answers it runs with `persistence: 'memory'` (nothing stored: each page load is a new anonymous visitor, so unique-visitor counts before consent are inflated). "Accept all" switches to `localStorage+cookie`, calls `identify(<player id>)` at a table (`useIdentifyPlayer`) and starts session recording; "Reject optional" or a withdrawal goes back to memory and sweeps the SDK's keys (`keys.ts`). "Manage preferences" can allow the identifier without the recording (the answer `analytics`: the SDK keeps its persistent storage and identifies, `disable_session_recording` stays on); taking the recordings back later stops the recording alone, taking the analytics back is a withdrawal, since a recording hangs on the identifier (`driver.ts`, `allowedOf` of `consent.ts`). `game_opened_from_hub` is counted on `/` and `/games` (the hub's two lists of games) from the links that carry `data-game="<slug>"` (the cards of the live games, which lead to the game's page on the hub: a new link from the hub to a game needs the attribute). The card of a game to come has none, and neither have the "Create a game" button and the cards of the related games of `/game/<slug>`, which the listener (mounted on `/` only) does not see
- The answer is `pbgo-consent` in `localStorage` (choice and date), asked again after 13 months (`consent.ts`). The banner (`CookieBanner` of `@pbgo/ui/platform`) is a wide card that floats over the bottom of the screen, centred and 896 px wide at most (the width of the screen less its margin below that): a title (`texts.label`, also the name of the dialog) and one sentence that ends with a "Privacy policy" link to `/legal#cookies` (the policy's own page, `/privacy`, is a stub for now), and three small buttons (`sm`) on one line, from the least to the most pushed: Reject optional (`ghost`), Manage preferences (`outline`) and Accept all (FR/EN/ES/DE in `texts.ts`, the English being the owner's; `texts.test.ts` caps the sentence and the words). From `lg` the words and the buttons are side by side, like a bar; under it the buttons are below the words, to the right, and on a phone they share the width of the card (two on a line, then the last). Manage preferences swaps the card for the choices one by one, in switches: the essential cookies (always on), the analytics (the identifier kept on the device) and the session recordings, which need the analytics (turning the analytics off turns them off); nothing is pre-ticked, a reopened banner opens on the stored answer, Back returns to the card and Save keeps the switches. The answer is one of three, `accepted` (both), `analytics` (the identifier without the recording) and `refused` (neither): `allowedOf` and `choiceOf` of `consent.ts` turn one into what the SDK may do and back, and `cookie_consent` reports it. The card is about 134 px high on a desktop (the same in French), so with its margin it keeps 18% of a 1440×900 screen free (21% of a 1024×768 one); 154 px in a tablet 768 px wide, where the buttons are under the words, and 222 to 241 px on a phone, which is 28% to 30% of a 390×844 screen (the third button costs a line). With the preferences open it is 321 px on a desktop and 474 on a phone, where it scrolls once it would be taller than the window: a shorter sentence is the lever. A visitor who already answered and reopens it gets a close button (Escape closes it too, once it has left the preferences) that keeps the answer, and the focus on the card. The full disclosure (what is counted either way, what a refusal stores) lives in `/legal#cookies`, not in the card. The banner and the "Cookies" links that reopen it (`ConsentLink`: hub footer, game home footer; the settings menu row) are hidden on pages that do not report: `/gamemaster`, `/design` and `/<slug>/preview/*` (`isExcludedPath`). The banner is `ph-no-capture` (its clicks are reported once, by `cookie_consent`, never by autocapture under the identity being given up; `banner.test.ts` keeps the class). What the visitor is told lives in `texts.ts` and in `/legal#cookies` (`(hub)/legal/page.tsx`): change them together with the behaviour
- The banner keeps its room free (`reserve.ts`, `reserveSpace`): it measures its outer box, the card plus the margin around it (a `ResizeObserver`), and publishes that height as `--consent-h` on `<body>`, with an inline `padding-bottom` of the same height, and removes both when it goes away (0px without a banner). Never on `:root`: the platform sets no value there, or every game would inherit it. A page that scrolls simply ends above the card; a screen as tall as the viewport and an element fixed to the bottom do the arithmetic themselves (see `docs/game-ui.md`). On a wide screen the card is narrower than the strip it reserves, which is the price of measuring one box. The table canvas and the board area resize when the banner appears, is reopened from a "Cookies" link or closes
- Recordings mask every input; the chat is `ph-no-capture`, nicknames printed by core are `ph-mask` (the tables draw theirs in a canvas, which is not recorded). A game that prints a nickname in its own DOM adds `ph-mask` there, and `ph-no-capture` as well when it sits inside a button or a link: autocapture sends the text of a clicked `a` / `button` (`$el_text`), nested text included. Never set `maskTextFn` in `options.ts`: it replaces the stars of every masked node and would show the nicknames
- Table codes (`/<slug>/game/<CODE>`, `/api/<slug>/games/<CODE>/…`, and `Table #XXX-XXX` of the tab title) are scrubbed by `scrub.ts`: from every property of an event (`before_send`), from the page address, the links and the network timings of a replay (`maskCapturedNetworkRequestFn`, `maskAttributeFn`), and the `<title>` is masked in the replay (`maskTextSelector: "title"`). The code field is `ph-mask` too. A new place that prints the code needs the same care. The pattern is any six letters or digits after `/game/` or `/games/`, so the hub's `/game/<slug>` of a six-letter slug (`/game/hanabi`) is reported as `/game/:code`: a slug is public, so nothing leaks, that page is just less precise in the reports. The client's masking options win over the project's "Privacy and masking" settings: leave the project's text and block selectors empty. The recorder compresses its larger chunks (gzip), so check a replay after gunzipping it, never by searching the raw upload
- Not in the repo, set in the PostHog project `pbgo` (EU cloud): recording enabled for the domain, exception autocapture, web vitals, IP anonymisation, the internal/test-account filter, authorised URLs
