# Guidelines

The tokens and the components are in [Foundations](../02-foundations/index.md) and [Components](../03-components/index.md), what the games share in [In-game UI](../04-in-game.md).

- [Principles](01-principles.md): the look of the platform and the rules every screen follows.
- [Layers and boundary](02-layers-and-boundary.md): the platform layer and the game layer, and what keeps them apart.
- [Adding a component](03-adding-a-component.md): the steps, and the tests that guard the rules.
- [In-game rules](04-in-game-rules.md): what holds for the UI that core draws inside a game.
