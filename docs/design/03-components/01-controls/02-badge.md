# Badge

Module: `@pbgo/ui/platform/badge`

A short label: a status, a count, a category. Never an action; use a `Button` for that. `notification` is the count of what waits for someone (messages not read, requests to answer), solid red. `destructive` and `success` are the signal colours as a dark tint: a loss and a win. `ranked` is shiny gold, the mark of a table played for the rank; its light stands still when motion is reduced.
