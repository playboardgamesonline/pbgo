# Radio group

Module: `@pbgo/ui/platform/radio-group`, `@pbgo/ui/platform/label`

One choice among a few, all shown at once: `RadioGroup` holds the choice and each `RadioGroupItem` is one of them, paired with a `Label`. A [select](05-select.md) is for a long list and a [toggle group](10-toggle-group.md) for a choice that sits in a bar.

- A radio is round, as the mark of a choice always is.
- The label is part of the target: pressing it picks the choice.
- The arrow keys move the choice and Tab leaves the group.
