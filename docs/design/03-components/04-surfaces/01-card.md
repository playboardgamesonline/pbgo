# Card

Module: `@pbgo/ui/platform/card`

The container of the platform: a `bg-card` surface with a hairline ring and [`shadow-surface`](../../02-foundations/05-shadows-and-rings.md), rounded to [`rounded-4xl`](../../02-foundations/03-radius.md). Header, content and footer share one `--card-spacing`; `size="sm"` tightens it.

## Default and small

`CardAction` sits at the top right of the header. The `sm` size is the compact card, for a short list of facts.

```specimen
card-default-and-small
```
