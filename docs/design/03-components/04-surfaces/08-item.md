# Item

Module: `@pbgo/ui/platform/item`

One row of a list, in an outlined frame: a media, a title and a description, and actions on the right. Use it for settings and the rows of a page; reach for a [card](01-card.md) when the content is a block of its own. The picture of a game in it is a tile (`rounded-tile`).

## Sizes

`default` and `sm` trade padding for density.
