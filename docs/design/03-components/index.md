# Components

The pieces of the platform, live, in five groups by what they do. A screen of the hub, the account, the legal pages or the 404 is built from these and from nothing else, and a piece that is missing is added to `@pbgo/ui` and to a page here before it is used ([Adding a component](../01-guidelines/03-adding-a-component.md)).

- [Controls](01-controls/index.md): what a visitor presses, types in or picks: buttons, badges, fields, checkboxes and switches, selects, comboboxes, sliders, meters, tabs and toggle groups.
- [Navigation and brand](02-navigation/index.md): the bars that take a visitor from page to page, and the logo.
- [Overlays](03-overlays/index.md): what appears over the page: dialogs, menus, popovers, hover cards, toasts and the cookie banner.
- [Surfaces and data](04-surfaces/index.md): the containers and what they hold: cards, games, avatars, alerts, progress, lists, empty states and tables.
- [Social](05-social/index.md): the friends of a signed-in visitor: the panel, the bar and the dock that turns from one into the other.
