# Design system

The two token layers of `@pbgo/ui` (platform and game), the palette, and the rules for components. The page `/design` shows every token and component: check it before building a screen.

## Design system (`@pbgo/ui`)
Black and white, dark theme only, Geist Sans in semibold with tight line-height and letter-spacing. No accent colour, and a single gradient, neutral too: the backdrop of the home's hero (`bg-linear-to-b from-secondary via-card to-background`), lighter at the top and black where the games start. Game art is the only colour on the platform. Every token and component is shown on the internal page `/design` (`(hub)/design/page.tsx`, noindex, not in the sitemap): it is the reference, check it before building a screen.
- Two layers share the token names (`--background`, `--foreground`, `--primary`, `--muted`, `--border`, `--radius`, `--font-body`…):
  - Platform layer (hub, account, legal, admin, 404): values in `packages/ui/src/styles/platform.css`, scoped to the class `.platform`. The class sits on `<html>` (hub and 404, through `PlatformDocument`), on the admin wrapper and on the root of every portal (select, dropdown, dialog, sheet, tooltip, toast), which React renders outside the wrapper. Components: `@pbgo/ui/platform/*`
  - Game layer: the `:root` values of `packages/core/styles/game.css`, overridden at runtime by the game's Sanity skin (inline style on `<html>`, `themeStyle()`). Components: `@pbgo/ui/game/*`. A game changes its look by changing token values, never a component
- Boundary: the platform never sets a value on `:root` and a game never sets one on `.platform`. In `platform.css`, `@theme` only defines new names (`shadow-surface|float|overlay`, `duration-fast|base|slow`, `ease-standard`, `bg-scrim`, `max-w-page|reading`, `px-gutter`, `py-section`, `h-navbar|pt-navbar|scroll-mt-navbar`); a name Tailwind already has (type-scale line-heights, `--tracking-tight`, `--default-transition-*`, `--font-weight-*`) gets its platform value inside `.platform`, or every game would inherit it
- In-game UI: not part of the platform layer. It is drawn in the game's own look (its skin tokens `--foreground`, `--surface`, `--accent-game`, `--font-title`, `--font-accent`, set by `themeStyle()`), tuned by `UI_THEME` (see `docs/game-ui.md`); never `@pbgo/ui/platform`, Geist or the `.platform` class. `/design › In-game UI` is the one place where the hub shows it: two games (Courtisans and the template) side by side, each inside a `GameScope` (`_components/game-scope.tsx`) that resets the platform's values (Geist, weight, line-heights, tracking, radius) and sets the game's tokens and font stacks on its own wrapper, never on `:root`. The hub does not load `game.css`: `globals.css` points `@source` at the four pure components (`seat-label`, `players-strip-view`, `announcement-view`, `game-over-panel`) and maps the utilities only games have (`font-display`, `bg-surface`, `bg-surface-dark`, `text-accent-game`) with `@theme inline`, which emits no variable. A new pure component drawn on `/design` needs its `@source` there. The font files of a game are not loaded on the hub, so its families fall to the next of the stack (Georgia); the colours, layout and motion are the real ones. Use `data-look`, never `data-game` (analytics counts the hub links that carry it)
- Palette: Tailwind's `neutral` scale (chroma 0) and `destructive`, nothing else. One font weight (600): `font-medium`, `font-bold`… resolve to it, so don't write them. The exceptions are the display faces, the title of the home and the finest print (`fine-print`), which each set their own weight (below). Depth: `shadow-surface|float|overlay` and `ring-1 ring-foreground/10`. Motion: `duration-fast|base|slow`, `ease-standard`. Shapes: `rounded-*`, scaled from `--radius`
- Motion (section `motion` of `/design`): three durations (`duration-fast`, 100 ms: overlays entering and leaving; `duration-base`, 150 ms: hover and focus, the default of `transition`; `duration-slow`, 250 ms: things that travel), one easing (`ease-standard`, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`: quick to leave and slow to settle) and one spring, tokens of `platform.css`. Plain `transition` and `transition-colors` already use the base duration and the standard easing, so a component writes neither, and the guard test rejects `duration-200`. What CSS cannot do (a layout animation, a sequence, a spring) is done with `motion/react` (motion.dev, a dependency of `@pbgo/ui` and of `@pbgo/core`), which reads the same numbers from `@pbgo/ui/motion`: `DURATION` in seconds, `EASE_STANDARD` as the four numbers of the bezier, and `SPRING_TRAVEL` (stiffness 520, damping 42: a damping ratio of about 0.9, no overshoot worth seeing, settled in about a quarter of a second) for something that moves to a place it was not, the fill that glides between the navbar's tabs. `motion.test.ts` reads `platform.css` and fails when a copy has drifted. The games' UI shares the easing (`EASE_STANDARD` of `@pbgo/core/lib/motion-preference`) and has its own timings (`useMotion()`, `docs/game-ui.md`). Less motion is honoured everywhere: in CSS the three durations become 0ms under `prefers-reduced-motion`, in JavaScript `Navbar` mounts `MotionConfig reducedMotion=user` (the fill jumps) and `FaceCycle` does not rotate. The platform moves in JavaScript in two places only, the navbar's fill and the title's word; an animation answers an action or says that something changed, uses the tokens, and never has a duration or an easing written in a component
- Popovers (select, dropdown menu, popover) are translucent (`bg-popover/70`) and carry their blur on the popover element itself (`backdrop-blur-2xl backdrop-saturate-150`), never on a `::before`. A descendant with a blend mode (the `Avatar` ring is `mix-blend-lighten`) makes the popover an isolated group in Chromium, and a pseudo-element backdrop then blurs nothing: the page shows through the list, sharp. The game switcher (an `Avatar` in each of its rows) is the live check of this rule
- `PLATFORM_THEME_COLOR` (`@pbgo/ui/theme`) is the browser colour of platform pages; `theme.test.ts` keeps it equal to `--background`. `platform.test.ts` rejects `dark:`, palette and hard-coded colours, arbitrary sizes and the old `admin` names in `packages/ui/src/platform/*`
- The hub's game card is `GameCard` (`@pbgo/ui/platform/game-card`), built on `GameCardVisual`, its picture: the game's logo over its background on the `bg-muted` frame, no layer between the two (no scrim: the art shows as it is), the frame alone when there is neither. The page of a game on the hub draws the same `GameCardVisual`, larger, so the card and the page cannot drift apart. `/design` (section `game-card`) shows the card of a live game and of a game to come, then the picture alone
- The hub's navbar is `Navbar` (`@pbgo/ui/platform/navbar`), with `Logo` and `SearchBar` beside it: hand-written, not from shadcn, shown on `/design` (sections `logo`, `navbar`, `search-bar`). `Navbar` is two containers kept apart over a transparent bar, `h-navbar` high: `NavbarStart` on the left (the brand, then the tabs) and `NavbarEnd` on the right (the account: an avatar of `size-10`, or Sign in and Let's play as `lg` buttons). Each is a glass island, a rounded square (`h-14 rounded-2xl border bg-background/80 shadow-surface backdrop-blur-md`, its content `h-10` and `rounded-lg`: the island's radius less its `p-2`, so the corners stay concentric; `NavbarEnd` makes the buttons in it `rounded-lg` too, a button being a pill elsewhere) that takes the pointer back, while the bar between the two lets the page and the pointer through. The bar spans the window, not the page's `max-w-page`: the islands sit on its edges, `0.75rem` in from the top and from each side. The brand is the home's button, a `size-10` square around the `Logo` (`h-6`, the size of the tabs' text): `current` marks it on the front page, where no tab is, the way a tab is marked on its own page: the bar's fill (`bg-muted`) behind it, with the logo as white as ever. That fill is one element for the whole bar (`layoutId` of `motion/react`, `SPRING_TRAVEL` of `@pbgo/ui/motion`): when the current item changes it glides to the next instead of jumping. It can only do that if the bar stays mounted from one page to the next, so the hub's layout mounts it once (`SiteHeader` of `apps/web`, a client component that reads the address to know what is current) and the pages do not draw it. `Navbar` mounts `MotionConfig reducedMotion=user` and a `LayoutGroup` of its own, so a visitor who asks for less motion gets the jump and two bars on one page (the specimens of `/design`) do not trade the fill The bar is pinned (`fixed inset-x-0 top-0 z-40`) and the page leaves `pt-navbar` under it or lets the backdrop of its first screen run under the islands (the home's hero does), and gives an anchor that lands below it `scroll-mt-navbar`; the tabs leave the first island under `md`. `Logo` is an inline SVG on `currentColor`, since `apps/web/public` holds `favicon.ico` only. `SearchBar` draws the search bar and nothing else: the page owns the text and what it finds (the home lays the bar over the bottom of its title, in the hero: its top edge sits on the baseline of the last line, so the letters stand on it and the tails of the script letters pass behind it)
- The filters of the hub's search bar are segments in the bar (`SearchBarSegments` of `@pbgo/ui/platform/search-bar`), of two kinds. A `SearchBarSegment` is an icon and what is picked, dimmed while nothing is, in a button that opens something: a `Popover` (`@pbgo/ui/platform/popover`) for the category, whose panel holds a `Combobox` (`@pbgo/ui/platform/combobox`), a list with a search field above it (case and accents ignored; the arrow keys, Enter and a click pick or unpick, any number can be picked, so the list stays open), or a `DropdownMenu` for the age ("Any age", "+6 yrs" to "+14 yrs", one choice). No label is drawn (the icon says what it is; the `label` is what a screen reader hears before the value). A `SearchBarStepper` is a number (players, duration) and has no panel: the icon, a minus button, the number and a plus button, with no label (the icon says what the number is; the label only names the group for a screen reader), the two buttons outlined, and no background under the pointer. Like the navbar they are hand-written, not from shadcn, and shown on `/design` (sections `search-bar`, `combobox`, `popover`). With segments the bar has two floors at any width, the field on top and the filters under it, with no line between them and no frame or separator between the filters: compact controls packed to the left, 1.5rem apart (2rem from 48rem of room), that go on a second row when the room runs out, so the text of the field and the icons of the filters start on the same line. The bar has round sides. Up to 48rem of room (asked of its container, `@container` and `@3xl`, not the window, so the same bar fits the home's hero and a narrow column) it is a card with large corners (`rounded-4xl`) and the submit button, 40px high with `rounded-2xl` corners, ends the field. From 48rem (the home's bar is `max-w-3xl`) it is a pill (`rounded-full`): the field and the filters are one block on the left, with room for the curve of the end (`pl-9`), and the button (`size-23`, a circle as tall as the two floors, the field's 48px, 4px and the filters' 40px, concentric with the end of the pill and 8px from its edge) stands alone on the right. The panel is portaled, so like every portal it carries the `platform` class itself, and it sits at `z-50`, above the navbar's `z-40`. A chip that is picked is `bg-primary`; the number of a stepper is one: dimmed while nothing is picked, `bg-primary` once it is, and pressing it picks it or clears it. The first press on either button picks the number the stepper shows (3 players, 30 minutes: `FIRST_PICKS` of `@pbgo/catalog/filter`), and then each press moves one place along the options (`stepOption`), down to the first or up to the last, which the buttons stop at. The category segment is always drawn: with no category yet its panel says so. The bar is glass, like the navbar's islands: `bg-background/60` and `backdrop-blur-xl`, so whatever is behind it (the foot of the home's title) shows through, blurred; its corners are `rounded-4xl` (a pill without segments) and its submit button is `secondary`, not the white of a primary action. The filters' icons are the ones the game's card and page already use: `UserMultipleIcon` for the players, `HourglassIcon` for the duration, `ChildIcon` for the age (and `GridViewIcon` for the category). The home's field says what the bar is for, "Search games, tables & tournaments...": only the games can be searched today (tables and tournaments have no list yet), and the field is `text-base` until the bar has 32rem of room (`@lg`, then `text-lg`) while its placeholder, which iOS does not zoom for, steps down to `text-sm` and, under 21rem of room, to `text-xs`, so the sentence fits a phone from 360 px wide
- The title of the home is three lines of capitals in Geist Black, all the same size (`text-display`): `display-title` (`platform.css`), weight 900 (the one weight the platform sets on its own for text: Geist's variable font has it, everything else is 600), a line-height of 0.9, the tight tracking of the headings (set on each line too: an `em` is inherited as a length, so a line bigger than the title would keep the title's) and the full colour of the text at the top, fading to nothing at the bottom (a mask over the whole title, `mask-image` from `foreground` to transparent, so that the pattern and the backdrop show through more and more). BOARD GAMES, W/ FRIENDS and & RIVALS are not the same width, on purpose: 7.30em, 5.85em and 4.41em in that face, so the title narrows as it goes down. The size follows the window (`7.7vw + 0.5rem` up to 7.5rem: the first line takes 71% of a 390 px window and 60% of a 1440 px one). `-mt-title-overlap` pulls the bar up by 0.3em: the 0.1em that a line of that line-height keeps under its baseline, and 0.2em more (a little more than a quarter of its capitals' height), so the bar covers the foot of the last line, which is already fading out and shows through its glass. The hero has a grid of thin lines behind the title (`pattern-grid` in `platform.css`: lines of one pixel, 3rem apart, in a faint `foreground`, on a layer of its own faded out towards the foot by a mask: `<div aria-hidden="true" className="pattern-grid pointer-events-none absolute inset-0 mask-b-from-30%" />` in a `relative overflow-hidden` section whose content is `relative`). It had a faint backdrop of game pieces behind the title (`BoardPattern`, `@pbgo/ui/platform/board-pattern`, section `board-pattern`: a tile of line icons that repeats, drawn at full strength in `foreground` and made faint with the `opacity` of the whole drawing, faded out towards the foot by a mask); it is not drawn on the home for now, the component and its section stay, and putting it back is one line in the hero (`<BoardPattern className="absolute inset-0 text-foreground opacity-8 mask-b-from-30%" />` in a `relative overflow-hidden` section whose content is `relative`). The face is meant to be able to change: it is `display-title`, the size (it follows the widths of the face's lines) and, for a face that is not Geist, the import of its font, nowhere else. `FaceCycle` (`@pbgo/ui/platform/face-cycle`, section `face-cycle` of `/design`) is the other way to set a title, kept for when the rotation comes back: one word of a line changes between four display faces every six seconds (`display-unkempt`, `display-jersey`, `display-fraktur`, `display-birthstone`, listed in `faces.ts`), out and in with `DURATION.slow` and `EASE_STANDARD`, the rest of its line gliding when it changes width; with `prefers-reduced-motion` it does not rotate. The display faces are the typefaces the logo's four letters are drawn in; their fonts are Fontsource packages (`@fontsource/*`, the latin subset) that `apps/web/src/app/globals.css` imports, so a game page never carries them. Each sets its own weight, since the platform's 600 on a face that has none would be a fake bold, and a scale of the parent's size that gives it the cap height of Geist's capitals (measured, not guessed). A title stays one text, written as it is (the capitals are CSS), for a screen reader and a search engine. It is for a title from `text-3xl` up
- The blocks of the home are `Section` (`@pbgo/ui/platform/section`, section `section` of `/design`): `Section` (as wide as the page, `max-w-page` and `px-gutter`, with `pb-section` below it, so a stack of them needs no spacing of its own), `SectionPreview` (a list that has more to show than the section has room for: what it holds fades out at the bottom, a `mask-b-from-60%` on the content alone, so it is opacity and no colour of the page, and its `action` sits over the fade, centred) and `SectionAction` (the way to the page of the tab: a link drawn as a button, bigger than a control of a page, `h-12` and `text-base`, since it is the one thing a visitor is asked to do there; the framework's link through `asChild`). A section has no visible title, what it shows says what it is, and a screen reader gets an `sr-only` heading. The home cuts its grid of popular games to two rows at every width (the cards beyond them are `hidden`, so they are neither drawn nor reached by the keyboard), and a section with nothing to list yet is a compact `Empty` (`p-6`) with the same button under it. `cn` knows the platform's own spacing names (`gutter`, `section`, `navbar`, `title-overlap`), so a `px-0` replaces a `px-gutter` the way it would a `px-4`; before that tailwind-merge kept both and the order of the generated CSS decided, which `utils.test.ts` now keeps from coming back
- The footer's backdrop is `Wordmark` (`@pbgo/ui/platform/wordmark`, section `wordmark`): the letters PBGO as SVG text in the four faces the logo is drawn in (the P `display-fraktur`, the B `display-unkempt`, the G `display-jersey`, the O `display-birthstone`), scaled by its viewBox, so always the width of its container. Each letter is placed by hand from its measured ink (a canvas `measureText` at 360 units, with the faces' own scales; measure again when a face changes), not from the advance of its glyph, which left the P and the B almost touching and a wide gap between the B and the G: the boxes of the ink of two letters are 20 units apart, except the P and the B: the P leaves much air at its lower right, so the B is 50 units closer, which makes the closest row of their shapes 19.5 units apart, like the other pairs (measured on the letters as drawn), the ink of the P starts at the left edge of the viewBox and the ink of the O ends at its right edge, and the letters are aligned on the middle of their ink, since the P and the O have a tail under the baseline that the B and the G do not. It fades from the colour of the footer's text (`--muted-foreground`) at 60%, a softer grey than the text, at the top of the letters to nothing at their foot (an SVG gradient in the units of the viewBox, over the whole height of the letters), and is `aria-hidden`: a drawing behind the text. `SiteFooter` (`apps/web`) is as wide as the site and draws, from the top, one row with the copyright and who built the site on the left (`COPYRIGHT_YEAR`, `COPYRIGHT_NAME` and `AUTHOR` of `@/site`, the author's name a link to their site) and the Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy (the cookies section of the legal notice) and Terms of Use links on the right (the first and the last are `LegalStubPage`s until their text is written; the Cookie settings link is there where analytics are on), then the wordmark from edge to edge, with no line anywhere, and, alone and centred under it, what the site is not (an unofficial adaptation: the games belong to their authors and publisher), the finest print of the page (`fine-print`: `text-xs`, a regular weight (400) and `foreground` at 20%, so dark that it is hard to read), `mt-8` under the letters and with `pb-5` under it
