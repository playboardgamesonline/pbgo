# Courtisans Online

Online version of the board game **Courtisans** (Catch Up Games), served by the single app `apps/web` at `/courtisans` on top of `@pbgo/core`.
Detailed architecture (intentions, tuning, pitfalls): `ARCHITECTURE.md`, a snapshot from before the move to core; it keeps the old French identifiers and paths, which are outdated.

## Code conventions
- English everywhere: identifiers, files, engine values, Sanity fields, comments. Player-facing texts, in every language, live in Sanity only (`texts`, `family`, `role`, `mission`), never in the code: a text Sanity does not have shows as `[missing: <key>]`
- Glossary: courtisan → `courtier`, famille → `family` (`butterfly`, `toad`, `nightingale`, `hare`, `stag`, `carp`), rôle → `role` (`noble`, `spy`, `assassin`, `guard`), domaine → `domain`, table de la Reine → `table` (levels `up` = light / `down` = disgrace), statut → `status` (`light`, `disgrace`, `neutral`), pioche → `deck`, main → `hand`, tapis → `mat`, siège → `seat`, journal → `log`, partie → `game`

## Assets
Nothing of this game is served from `apps/web/public`: every picture file and every sound comes from Sanity (`game`, `family`, `role`, `courtier`, `mission`, `interface`, `settings`, `audio`). The code holds colours and the sound mix (`src/lib/sounds.ts`: volume and spacing of each effect; an effect with no file in Gamemaster › Audio stays silent). A picture that is missing is drawn as a plain stand-in on the table and stays visible: the `[textures]` report in the browser console, the Launch checklist and the "No file in Sanity" badge of Gamemaster › Audio.

## Structure
- `package.json` — `@pbgo-game/courtisans`, the binding package loaded by `@pbgo/games`
  - `src/index.ts` (engine `GAME`, site constants and `ERROR_CODES`, `SOUNDS`, `DEFAULT_SKIN`, `UI_THEME`, `SETTINGS_DEFAULTS`), `src/ui.tsx` (`Logo`, `Game` loaded on demand), `src/server.ts` (`loadGameData` = catalog with the board's words), `src/runtime.tsx` (fonts, `styles.css`, `BindingProvider`)
  - `src/components/game` — board (`game.tsx`: opening, interactions, announcements, end), messages, pictograms, score detail
    - opening: the banquet waits until every player has read their missions (engine phase `missions`: `GAME.waitingFor` = the players who have not read, `GAME.autoPlayFor` reads for one of them). After the idle delay the players present can vote to read for the latecomers: core's `StalledTurn` with the `missionsSkip*` labels, `POST /api/courtisans/games/<code>/takeover`; the opening has no route of its own
  - `src/components/board` — react-three-fiber scene (`layout.ts` computes the poses, `scene.tsx` moves each card to its pose)
  - `src/lib/catalog.ts` (the catalog's types, the fields of the board's vocabulary and the catalog shown when Sanity answers nothing: every text a `[missing: …]` marker, no picture; the pictures and texts come from Sanity), `src/lib/skin.ts` (colours of the banquet skin, never an image or a text), `src/lib/sounds.ts` (the sound mix; the files are in Sanity)
  - `src/lib/ui-theme.ts` — `UI_THEME`, how the shared in-game UI keeps the banquet look: announcements in the accent font (uploaded in Gamemaster › Visual), white with a gold outline and gold hairlines, gold star confetti; round seat discs with a pale ring and the title font; an end screen on the navy surface with a gold line and a gold accent; `motion: "lively"` (confetti, springs, emoji burst; reduced motion still wins). The look changes there, never in a copy of a shared component
- `apps/studio` — Sanity Studio on `@pbgo/studio-kit` (the game's own `texts` replaces the shared version)
- `packages/engine` — pure TypeScript engine (`@courtisans/engine`) on the engine-kit helpers, `GAME` = `@pbgo/engine-kit` contract, tested with Vitest (see Engine)
  - `src/conformance.test.ts` (the contract of an engine, played the way the server plays it), `src/takeover.test.ts` (absent players and the votes to play for them, on core's rig) and `src/error-codes.test.ts`

## Engine (`packages/engine/src`)
- `types.ts` (`State`, `Action`, the events `CourtisansEvent`, the views, the results, `COURTISANS_ERROR_CODES`) · `catalogue.ts` (the 90 cards: `FAMILIES`, `ROLES`, `CARDS`, `CATALOGUE`, `weight`) · `missions.ts` (`MISSIONS`, the twenty missions with their rules, and `evaluateCondition`) · `machine.ts` (`open`/`close` of a step on the kit, the cards, `phaseOf`, `waitingFor`, `progress`) · `scoring.ts` · `results.ts` · `actions.ts` (`step`) · `setup.ts` · `view.ts` · `autoplay.ts` · `debug.ts` · `game.ts` (`GAME`) · `index.ts` (the kit's exports too)
- `test-utils.ts` — `layout` builds a rigged table from the names of the cards (`card("hare", "noble")`), `problems` checks the invariants of a state; not exported

### How a game goes
- `step(state, action)` gives `{ state, events }`: the state is lean (ids and numbers: `seats`, `ids`, `rng`, `deck`, `setAside`, `eliminated`, `hands`, `domains`, `table`, `missions`, `read`, `active`, `zones`) and what happened (a card played, a card eliminated, cards drawn, the end) goes to the log of the table, which the board reads with `useGameLog`. The phase is read from the state (`phaseOf`): the missions are read, then the banquet, then the game is over once no hand holds a card
- The opening is a simultaneous step: every player says they have read their missions (`readMissions`, once is enough), the banquet starts when the last has. `waitingFor` lists the players who have not, then the player of the turn; `autoPlayFor` reads for one absent player or plays their whole turn
- A turn: the player plays a card in each of three zones at most, once each: the Queen's table (above the mat, the light, or below it, the disgrace), their own domain, another player's domain (`zones`). An assassin may eliminate a card of the zone it is played in, a guard never, itself never. The turn ends at three zones or when the hand is empty: the player draws back to three and the next player who holds a card is up; when nobody does the game is over
- The box is 90 cards, 15 for each of the six families (4 nobles, 2 spies, 2 assassins, 3 guards, 4 with no role), shuffled with the seed; 30, 18, 6 or none are put aside at 2, 3, 4 or 5 players, each player is dealt three, the first player is drawn from the seed and each is dealt a white and a blue mission
- A family is in the light when its cards above the mat weigh more than those below (a noble weighs two), in disgrace when they weigh less and neutral at a tie. A player's domain is worth, for each family it holds, its weight times 1 in the light, -1 in disgrace and 0 when neutral, and each mission that holds adds 3. The best total wins and a tie shares the win
- Hidden: the hands of the others, the order of the deck, the cards put aside, the two missions of each player until the end, and the family of a spy that was played (to its owner too), which the log never says either. A card is an opaque id (`ids`); what it is comes from the catalogue
- The automatic moves draw from the stream the state carries (`rng`): the same state gives the same moves, and a move a player sends draws nothing

### Tests
`pnpm --filter @courtisans/engine test`: a file per part (setup, actions, missions, scoring, view, autoplay, determinism, game), `simulation.test.ts` (2 to 5 players, random legal moves, the invariants after each, the points written again from the names of the cards) and `golden.test.ts`: ten whole games played by a scripted bot through the public view, written before the engine moved onto the kit, which hold what each game ends on (the plays, the turns, the standing of each family, each player's points and missions). They must not change unless a rule does

## Sanity
- Project `2lo2f5sv`, dataset `production`, studio `courtisans.sanity.studio`; admin writes use `SANITY_API_WRITE_TOKEN_COURTISANS`
- Shared (studio-kit): `settings` (logo, theme, fonts, credits; the accent font is the file uploaded in Gamemaster › Visual), `interface` (top/bottom decor, character = the Queen, also the cover of the hub card, pattern, host pictogram, player colors), `texts` (interface labels, victory sentences + `missionsButton`, `banquetStarts`, the two status wordings and, in its Board tab, the 17 words of the table)
- Game-specific: `game` (tabs Board: mat and its texture, Card backs, Arrows), `family`, `role` (name, key, copies per family, pictogram, lettering: no `Rule` field), `courtier`, `mission` (a text and a card: its colour and its rule are the engine's, `MISSIONS`, found by the id of the document). No `rules` document: the rules are PDF links, set in `settings` (Gamemaster › Mechanics)
- Queries in `src/sanity/queries.ts`, then `pnpm --filter courtisans-studio typegen`

## Content (Sanity)
Everything the players read, see or hear is in Sanity, in every language (GAM-20, GAM-22, GAM-23): the name of the 6 families, the name of the 4 roles, the text of the 20 missions, the `texts` singleton (victory sentences, the interface labels, the 12 error messages, the missions button and the start of the banquet, the two status wordings and the 17 words of the board, in its Board tab; its tagline, home title and introduction, hidden home heading and genres are not read since the game has no home page: it is opened on the platform's page, PLT-106), the pictures, the sounds and the accent font (Typey, `settings.accentFontFile`). The code holds none of it: a text Sanity does not have reads `[missing: <field>]` on the page and is logged once per language on the server, a picture it does not have is drawn as a plain stand-in. The twenty missions themselves are the engine's (`MISSIONS`: the colour and the rule of each), Sanity holds only their texts and their pictures, found by the id. `src/lib/site.ts` keeps the name, the author, the contact, the keywords and the list of error codes (their messages are in Sanity)
- The seed that wrote the translations (GAM-20, GAM-22) is gone: Sanity is the record, edited in the Studio and in the gamemaster. The documents are never created from code
- `mission-dark-8`, `-9` and `-10` have their English, Spanish and German as drafts, written from the Studio's French, for the owner to read and publish. Until they are, a player who reads English (the default), Spanish or German sees `[missing: missions.mission-dark-8]` (and `-9`, `-10`) on those three cards: a text reads the language asked, then English, never French (PLT-103). `mission-dark-10` is checked by a condition (in the engine) that counts the cards below the mat in total (no family filter), while its French text says "of each family", and two more missions read differently from their text or their siblings (`mission-dark-9` counts weight where its text says cards, `mission-light-5` counts cards where its five siblings count weight): all three transcribed as Sanity had them, to settle with the owner
- The body font is Alegreya Variable from npm (`src/styles.css`); titles follow it. Only the accent font is a file in Sanity
